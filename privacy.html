Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-28

"Content Automation Pipeline" ("the Application") is a personal automation tool operated by its developer. This policy explains what data the Application accesses and how it is used.

No third-party user data

The Application does not collect, process, or share personal data belonging to any third-party user. It is not a public-facing service and does not have end users other than its developer.

Data accessed

The Application connects, via OAuth, only to social media accounts that are owned and explicitly authorized by the developer. It uses this access solely to publish video content generated by the developer to those same accounts.

Data storage

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the developer's own machine and are never transmitted to or shared with any third party.

No advertising or resale of data

The Application does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties, and does not use any data for advertising purposes.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the repository owner via github.com/nknaeppe.
